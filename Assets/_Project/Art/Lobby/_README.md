# Lobby Art Intake

Temporary and final lobby assets live here. Keep source files outside this folder unless they are approved for Unity import.

## Naming

- Background: `lobby_bg_<name>_temp.png` or `lobby_bg_<name>_v###.png`
- Logo: `lobby_logo_<name>_temp.png` or `lobby_logo_<name>_v###.png`

## Import Settings

- Texture Type: Sprite (2D and UI)
- Backgrounds: 1080x1920 or larger portrait-friendly art
- Logos: transparent PNG, preserve aspect
- Compression: use settings that do not produce visible mobile UI artifacts

## Safe Area

- Keep the center readable for title/logo.
- Keep top-left clear enough for profile card.
- Keep lower-left or bottom area clear enough for main buttons.
- Do not place critical art under mobile notches or bottom gesture areas.
