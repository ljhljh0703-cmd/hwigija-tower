# Asset Intake Checklist

Use these folders when importing contractor or generated assets.

## Visuals

- `Assets/_Project/Art/Characters/`
- `Assets/_Project/Art/Environments/`
- `Assets/_Project/Art/Nodes/`
- `Assets/_Project/Art/UI/`
- `Assets/_Project/Art/Icons/`
- `Assets/_Project/Art/VFX/`

## Audio

- `Assets/_Project/Audio/Music/`
- `Assets/_Project/Audio/SFX/`
- `Assets/_Project/Audio/Ambience/`
- `Assets/_Project/Audio/Voice/`

## Unity Assembly Assets

- `Assets/_Project/Prefabs/`
- `Assets/_Project/Animations/`
- `Assets/_Project/Materials/`
- `Assets/_Project/VFX/`
- `Assets/_Project/UI/`

## Rules

- Keep final imported assets under `Assets/_Project`, not loose under `Assets/`.
- Keep raw naming role-first and stable: `Character_Player_Base`, `SFX_UI_Select_01`.
- Do not overwrite existing `.meta` files.
- Commit imported art/audio with generated `.meta` files.
